Account privacy

Updated September 11, 2026.

Your account information

When you create a Broadway Pixels account, we use your email address, player name, account identifier, and authentication records to provide sign-in, recovery, security, and connected game services. Our authentication service providers process passwords securely. Do not share your password with support.

Connected game progress

Optional Fishadise cloud saves are associated with your account and contain your game progress. The website shows when your cloud save was last updated. The same account connects your game and website profile. Apple or Game Center sign-ins can be linked to your game account; adding a website login keeps the same account identifier.

Your choices

In Your account, you can edit your player name, verify or change your email, reset your password, and request account deletion. Deletion removes the login and Fishadise cloud save. Local game progress remains on your device. Delete Apple-linked accounts in Fishadise so Apple access can also be revoked. Signing out or disabling sync does not delete your account or cloud save.

Retention and support

Account information is retained while your account is active. Service providers may retain limited security and operational records according to their retention policies. Contact support@broadwaypixels.com for access, correction, export, deletion, or account support. We may verify that a request concerns your account.

Read the Fishadise privacy policy for game services and the Broadway Pixels privacy policy for general website practices.
