Kitty Match Mania and Doggy Match Mania

Privacy policy

How Broadway Pixels, LLC handles information in these Android games.

Your information and choices

Last updated November 8, 2026.

Age and game progress

Both games can be played without an account. The age screen uses your birth date on your device and discards it after the check. Only the age classification or skipped result is saved on the device. Game progress and settings are saved locally. If you enable sync, the game also saves a cloud copy of your level stars and best scores.

Advertising and consent

Advertising is available only to players classified as adults. When ads are enabled, Google AdMob may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, device identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostics for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. Google provides consent and privacy choices where applicable. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Optional Facebook sync

Adults can choose to connect Facebook in Settings. The Facebook sign-in flow receives basic profile details such as your Facebook name and app-specific ID. Google Firebase authenticates the sign-in, may retain profile details supplied by Facebook, and associates your account identifier with level stars and best scores so the game can restore them on another device. You can disconnect in Settings to stop syncing on that device.

See the Meta Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.

Purchases and support

In Doggy Match Mania, Google Play processes in-game purchases and the game uses the purchase result to grant the item. The purchase system uses temporary diagnostics and performance data to operate and troubleshoot the store.

When you contact us, Broadway Pixels uses the name, reply address, and details you provide to handle your request. Our website privacy policy explains how support tickets are handled.

Retention and deletion

The game keeps your local save until you clear its storage or uninstall it. The cloud save is associated with your sync account. To remove the account and its cloud save, request sync account deletion. For other privacy questions, use Broadway Pixels support.
